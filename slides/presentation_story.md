# Presentation Story
## From Warming to Yield — Where Climate Risk Hits Agriculture First

### Slide 1 — The Business Problem
**Visual:** one strong crop/climate image + one sentence.
A global agribusiness client needs to know which crop-location combinations are becoming less reliable under climate pressure.

### Slide 2 — The Data Backbone
**Visual:** 4-source pipeline.
FAO crop yields → ERA5 climate → fertilizer → irrigation → country-year-crop panel.

### Slide 3 — Six Crops, One Question
**Visual:** small multiples of yield trends.
Show how Wheat, Maize, Rice, Potatoes, Soybeans and Barley evolved since 1990.

### Slide 4 — Warmer Than Usual Is the Better Question
**Visual:** crop faceted temperature-deviation plots.
Explain why comparing Egypt's raw temperature to France is less useful than asking what happens in a warmer-than-normal year within each country.

### Slide 5 — There Is No Magic Temperature
**Visual:** temperature-bin yield chart.
Direct callback to the old project: replace one exact “best degree” with ranges and uncertainty-aware interpretation.

### Slide 6 — Water and Management Change the Story
**Visual:** precipitation response + irrigation/fertilizer groups.
Separate uncontrollable climate exposure from potentially actionable management variables.

### Slide 7 — The Risk Matrix
**Visual:** 2D matrix: warming penalty × yield volatility.
Highlight the country-crop combinations the client should investigate first.

### Slide 8 — Can We Predict Later Years?
**Visual:** baseline vs model MAE and actual-vs-predicted plot.
Train on the past, test on 2018+.

### Slide 9 — Where V1 Fails
**Visual:** top error crops/countries.
A strong project explains failure modes instead of hiding them.

### Slide 10 — Client Action
**Visual:** 3-step decision playbook.
1. Prioritize high-risk crop-location pairs.
2. Validate with local growing-season/farm data.
3. Test irrigation/input adaptations before operational rollout.

### Final line
**Climate risk cannot be removed, but it can be measured earlier and prioritized better.**
